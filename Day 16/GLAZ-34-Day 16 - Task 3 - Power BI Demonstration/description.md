## Description

Perform an end-to-end Power BI hands-on exercise using multiple business datasets to understand how raw data is transformed into meaningful business intelligence.

The objective is to develop practical experience with ETL operations, data modeling, DAX calculations, semantic model queries, and interactive visualizations.

The final output should be a Power BI report that presents meaningful business insights and helps stakeholders make informed, data-driven decisions.

Scope of Work

1. Dataset Preparation
Identify and collect multiple related business datasets in CSV or Excel format.

Prepare datasets containing relevant business information, such as customers, orders, products, sales, and payments.

Inspect the datasets for missing values, duplicate records, inconsistent formats, and invalid entries.

Understand the relationships between the datasets.

1. Data Import and ETL
Import the datasets into Power BI Desktop.

Use Power Query Editor to perform ETL operations.

Extract the required columns and records.

Transform data by:

Removing duplicates and unnecessary columns.

Handling missing and invalid values.

Standardizing column names and data formats.

Converting columns to appropriate data types.

Creating calculated columns where required.

Merging and appending queries where appropriate.

Load the cleaned datasets into the Power BI data model.

1. Data Modeling and Relationships
Establish relationships between the imported tables.

Identify primary keys and foreign keys.

Configure appropriate cardinality and cross-filter directions.

Design a suitable Star Schema where applicable.

Separate fact tables and dimension tables.

Create and configure a dedicated Date/Calendar table.

1. Semantic Model Development
Build a reusable semantic model that represents the business data.

Create meaningful measures using DAX.

Implement business metrics such as:

Total Revenue

Total Orders

Average Order Value

Total Customers

Customer Retention or Repeat Purchase Rate

Month-over-Month Revenue Growth

Year-to-Date Revenue

Product and Category Performance

Use appropriate DAX functions, including CALCULATE, SUMX, FILTER, RELATED, DISTINCTCOUNT, and time-intelligence functions.

Organize measures into logical display folders and provide meaningful names.

1. DAX Queries and Semantic Analysis
Execute DAX queries against the semantic model using DAX Query View.

Retrieve summarized business data using EVALUATE and SUMMARIZECOLUMNS.

Apply filters and grouping to investigate business performance.

Validate DAX query results against report-level measures.

Investigate how filter context and row context affect calculations.

1. Business Intelligence and Visualization
Develop interactive Power BI report pages that communicate meaningful business information.

Suggested report pages:

Sales Overview

Total revenue and order count.

Revenue trends over time.

Average order value.

Sales performance by category.

Customer Analysis

Customer distribution by city or region.

New versus repeat customers.

Customer contribution to revenue.

High-value customer segments.

Product Performance

Revenue by product and category.

Best-performing and lowest-performing categories.

Product contribution to overall revenue.

Operational Analysis

Order status distribution.

Payment method analysis.

Order trends and operational exceptions.

Use appropriate visualizations such as KPI cards, bar charts, line charts, matrix visuals, slicers, and drill-through pages.
