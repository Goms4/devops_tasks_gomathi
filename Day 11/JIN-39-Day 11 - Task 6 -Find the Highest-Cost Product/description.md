## Description

Determine the product with the highest individual cost/price in the e-commerce dataset and validate the result.

Activities:

- Identify the appropriate product cost/price column.
- Ensure the column contains valid numeric values.
- Use .max() to find the highest product cost.
Example:


```
df['Product_Cost'].max()
```

- Identify the complete record corresponding to the maximum value.
- Verify the product name, category, order ID, and other relevant attributes.
- Check whether missing or incorrectly formatted values affect the result.
