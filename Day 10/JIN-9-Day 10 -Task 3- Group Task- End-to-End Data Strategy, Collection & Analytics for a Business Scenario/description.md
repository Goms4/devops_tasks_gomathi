## Description

Description:

As part of our data foundations and analytics upskilling, this task group focuses on understanding how organizations identify business problems, capture relevant data, process and analyze it, and convert the resulting insights into actionable decisions.

Choose a real-world business scenario from a domain such as e-commerce, banking, healthcare, retail, logistics, education, or product management. Based on the selected scenario, design an end-to-end data strategy that explains what data is required, how it can be captured, where it can be stored, how it can be processed and analyzed, and which tools and technologies are best suited for each stage.

The objective is to think beyond individual tools and understand the complete data lifecycle, from business requirements to data-driven decision-making.

Task Group Activities

Task 1: Identify the Business Scenario

- Concept: Understand the business problem and identify where data can provide value.
- Practice: Select a scenario, such as declining product sales, customer churn, delayed deliveries, fraudulent transactions, or low student engagement.
- Challenge: Define the problem statement, business objectives, stakeholders, and key questions that the analysis should answer.
Task 2: Identify the Data Requirements

- Concept: Determine which data points are necessary to understand and solve the problem.
- Practice: Identify relevant data entities, attributes, formats, and sources.
- Challenge: Prepare a data requirements table specifying the data field, its purpose, data type, source, and business relevance.
Task 3: Design the Data Capture Strategy

- Concept: Determine how data will be generated and collected from business activities.
- Practice: Explore data capture methods such as web forms, application logs, APIs, transactional databases, IoT sensors, surveys, CSV uploads, and third-party systems.
- Challenge: Explain how each required data point will be captured, when it will be collected, how frequently it will be updated, and who or what will generate it.
Task 4: Select Data Storage Solutions

- Concept: Choose suitable storage technologies based on data structure, volume, access patterns, and business requirements.
- Practice: Compare relational databases, NoSQL databases, data warehouses, data lakes, and cloud object storage.
- Challenge: Recommend a storage solution for the selected scenario and explain why it is appropriate.
Task 5: Plan Data Processing and Preparation

- Concept: Transform raw data into reliable, analysis-ready information.
- Practice: Identify data validation, missing-value handling, duplicate removal, standardization, integration, and transformation requirements.
- Challenge: Design a preprocessing workflow and identify suitable tools, such as SQL, Python, Pandas, Apache Spark, or ETL/ELT platforms.
Task 6: Choose Analytical Methods

- Concept: Decide how the prepared data will be analyzed to answer the business questions.
- Practice: Identify applicable analytical approaches, including descriptive, diagnostic, predictive, and prescriptive analytics.
- Challenge: Map each business question to an analytical method, required data, expected output, and potential business insight.
Task 7: Evaluate Tools and Technologies

- Concept: Select appropriate tools for each stage of the data lifecycle.
- Practice: Compare tools for data capture, integration, storage, processing, analytics, visualization, and automation.
- Challenge: Create a tool-selection matrix that includes the recommended tool, its purpose, advantages, limitations, cost considerations, and alternatives.
Task 8: Design Reporting and Decision-Making

- Concept: Communicate insights to stakeholders and connect analytical findings to business actions.
- Practice: Design a dashboard, report, or analytical output containing relevant KPIs, trends, comparisons, and recommendations.
- Challenge: Explain how decision-makers will use the output, what actions they could take, and how success will be measured.
Task 9: Address Data Quality, Security, and Governance

- Concept: Ensure that data is accurate, reliable, appropriately protected, and used responsibly.
- Practice: Identify potential risks involving inaccurate records, unauthorized access, sensitive information, retention, and data quality.
- Challenge: Propose appropriate validation rules, access controls, data-quality checks, privacy safeguards, and governance measures.
Task 10: Present the End-to-End Data Architecture

- Concept: Connect all the stages into a coherent data workflow.
- Practice: Create a flow diagram showing the movement of data from its original source through collection, processing, storage, analysis, and reporting.
- Challenge: Present the proposed architecture, justify the major technology choices, identify potential bottlenecks, and explain how the solution could scale as the business grows.
Expected Outcome

By completing this task group, you should be able to:

- Translate a business problem into clear data requirements.
- Identify suitable methods for capturing structured and unstructured data.
- Understand the roles of databases, data warehouses, data lakes, APIs, and data pipelines.
- Select appropriate technologies for processing, analysis, and visualization.
- Apply analytical methods to generate useful business insights.
- Evaluate data quality, security, scalability, and cost.
- Design an end-to-end data solution and explain the reasoning behind each technology choice.
Deliverables

1. Business scenario and problem statement.
2. Data requirements and data capture strategy.
3. Data source and storage selection matrix.
4. Data processing and analytical methodology.
5. Tool comparison and technology selection matrix.
6. Proposed dashboard, report, or analytical output.
7. Data quality, security, and governance considerations.
8. End-to-end data architecture diagram.
9. Final presentation explaining the solution and justifying the decisions.
Completion Criteria

The task group is complete when the selected scenario has a documented end-to-end data strategy, each major stage has an appropriate tool or technology recommendation, the analytical approach addresses the business problem, and the proposed solution is supported by clear reasoning.
