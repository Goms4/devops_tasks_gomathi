## Description

Description:

As part of our data foundations and analytics upskilling, this ticket focuses on selecting any five products from a domain of your choice and performing different types of data analysis to extract meaningful insights and support data-driven decision-making.

Choose a domain such as e-commerce, electronics, groceries, fashion, healthcare products, or any other suitable product-based industry. Create or collect a dataset containing relevant product information, clean and process the data, and apply the four major types of analytics: descriptive, diagnostic, predictive, and prescriptive analytics.

The Four Data Analytics Challenges

1. Descriptive Analytics: What Happened?

- Concept: Summarizes historical data to understand past performance, trends, and patterns.
- Practice: Calculate total sales, average product price, units sold, revenue by product, and best-selling products.
- Challenge: Analyze the five selected products and summarize their performance using tables, charts, or a dashboard.
2. Diagnostic Analytics: Why Did It Happen?

- Concept: Investigates the reasons behind observed patterns, changes, or unexpected results.
- Practice: Compare sales across products, examine pricing differences, and identify possible factors contributing to high or low sales.
- Challenge: Identify a performance gap, such as one product generating lower revenue than the others, and investigate possible reasons using the available data.
3. Predictive Analytics: What Might Happen?

- Concept: Uses historical data and statistical or machine-learning techniques to estimate future outcomes.
- Practice: Use historical sales data to forecast future demand, revenue, or product performance.
- Challenge: Build a basic forecasting model or statistical estimate for the five selected products. Document the assumptions and evaluate the prediction where sufficient historical data is available.
4. Prescriptive Analytics: What Should We Do?

- Concept: Recommends actions based on observed data, predicted outcomes, business objectives, and constraints.
- Practice: Use the analysis to suggest pricing adjustments, inventory changes, promotions, or product prioritization.
- Challenge: Recommend actionable strategies for improving product performance. Explain how the recommendations follow from the descriptive, diagnostic, and predictive findings.
Dataset Requirements

- Select any five products from a domain of your choice.
- Use a real dataset or create a realistic sample dataset.
- Include relevant attributes such as product name, category, price, units sold, revenue, date, inventory, and customer ratings, where applicable.
- For predictive analysis, use sufficient historical observations rather than relying only on five individual product records.
- Document missing values, data-cleaning steps, assumptions, and limitations.
